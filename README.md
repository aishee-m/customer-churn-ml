# Baseline Problem
A telecommunications company wants to identify customers who are at high risk of leaving so that its retention team can prioritize outreach to highest 10% of at risk customers. 

# Machine Learning Problem 
Given a customer account, billing information, and subscribed services, estimate their probability of churn. Output would be a churn probability between 0 and 1, an associated risk category and an optional explanation of important contributing features. 

# Dataset Selection
We have chosen the IBM Telco Customer Churn Data with 21 columns, and 7043 samples including the target and roughly 26.5% of positive churn cases. Data includes account, services, billing, demographic information with numerical and categorical features.
